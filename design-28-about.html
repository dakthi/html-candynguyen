ABOUT US

Talking and Exploring is not your usual tour operator or tour-guiding service. Each time, we offer a unique experience, depending on the topic, the location and the people who join us, each person bringing in their own perspective and encouraged to share their knowledge and experience.

"We are a tiny team devoted to our mission to bring people together to explore the lesser known areas (especially, but not only, of Ealing) and topics as well as to learn from and about one another."

Recent projects

See here a few pictures from the first part of our 'Remembering Katyń Victims' project in collaboration with Piotr Szkopiak, director of 'The Last Witness' and the Polish Social and Cultural Centre (POSK) in Hammersmith. The other part was a walk which started at the Katyń Memorial, through the Gunnersbury Cemetery, with a stop at a Gunnersbury Park cafe and a final stop at the Gunnersbury Museum. Our project was made possible owing to the support from the Polonia Aid Foundation Trust.

More footage from our events can be seen on the Ealing Walking Talking and Exploring Group on Facebook. Our upcoming events are published on the Talking and Exploring Eventbrite page and shared on other social media.

What We Do

We organise regular community walks that explore:

  • Local history and heritage sites
  • Black histories and cultural contributions
  • Street art and murals
  • Green spaces and parks
  • Community landmarks and hidden gems
  • Seasonal events and celebrations

Our Mission

We aim to:

  • Foster social cohesion and community connections
  • Celebrate diversity and promote inclusion
  • Preserve and share local stories and memories
  • Encourage physical activity and wellbeing
  • Support local organisations and initiatives

Work with us

Please get in touch if you would like to work with us. We welcome collaborations with local organisations, cultural centres, and community groups.

Join our community

All our walks are open to everyone, regardless of age, background, or fitness level. Whether you're a long-time resident or new to the area, you'll find a warm welcome and interesting conversations on our walks.

Most of our walks are free or donation-based (£5-£10 suggested) to keep them accessible to all. We often finish with optional refreshments at local cafés or community spaces.
